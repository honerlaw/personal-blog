Find jobs without the noise.

Seekless crawls company job boards, indexes the listings, and lets you search with plain language instead of keyword filters. It combines full-text search with vector similarity so results match what you mean, not just what you typed.

## How it works

- **Crawls job boards directly.** A Python pipeline pulls listings from company career pages and keeps them up to date.
- **Hybrid search.** Combines BM25 full-text matching with vector embeddings so you can search naturally and still get precise results.
- **Works everywhere.** Built on Expo, so it runs on web, iOS, and Android from a single codebase.

Live at [seekless.ai](https://www.seekless.ai).
